# Portfolio Module 02: Parachute Project Management, End-to-End SDLC Enablement & Ops-Tech Bridge Leadership (JPMorgan Chase)

## Executive Summary:
* **Domain:** Parachute Technical Project Management, SDLC Documentation Architecture, Operations Control Management (OCM) Alignment & Legacy Code Forensics
* **Company Context:** JPMorgan Chase & Co. (Wholesale Payments Operations)
* **Role:** Vice President – Intelligent Automation Lead & Ops-Tech Bridge Liaison
* **Core Value Delivered:** Rescued stalled and high-risk automation initiatives by stepping in as a "Parachute PM." Compressed delivery timelines by 30%–40%, reverse-engineered 28,000+ lines of undocumented legacy code to deliver $330K in direct cost avoidance, authored complete SDLC documentation suites (BRD, FSD, SDD, End User Guides), and architected the 2LOD CCOR Legal Entity risk coverage engine.

---

## 1. Operational Challenge & Project Bottlenecks:
* **Red-Status & Stalled Initiatives:** Critical automation projects frequently hit roadblocks due to vendor delays, complex system access hurdles, or technical skill gaps.
* **Legacy Risk & Un-Documented Code:** High-risk legacy User Developed Tools (UDTs) operating without documentation created severe compliance red flags and audit exposure.
* **Documentation Gaps & Misalignment:** Mismatches between frontline operational logic and engineering specs caused scope creep, UAT failures, and extended delivery cycles.
* **Governance & Handover Gaps:** Inadequate post-production monitoring, missing bot repository registrations, and unclear decommissioning/absorption roadmaps created long-term technical debt.

---

## 2. End-to-End SDLC Enablement & "Parachute PM" Rescue Framework:

### Execution Steps:
* **Step 1: Process Discovery & Requirement Architecture** – Sitting directly with Operations, mapping AS-IS/TO-BE workflows, and authoring precise Business Requirement Documents (BRD).
* **Step 2: Legacy Code Forensics & Risk Remediation** – Reverse-engineering undocumented legacy macros, reconstructing logic specifications, and executing risk remediation action plans.
* **Step 3: Bot Access Sourcing & Infrastructure Enablement** – Sourcing bot IDs and collaborating with competent authorities to secure UAT access.
* **Step 4: Hands-On Technical Guidance & UAT Execution** – Guiding engineering teams step-by-step through coding challenges across AA, UiPath, Alteryx, and Xceptor; uploading real-world test data and securing UAT sign-offs.
* **Step 5: Production Hypercare & Lifecycle Handover** – Executing hypercare monitoring, registering assets into the firmwide Bot Repository, and aligning solutions with technology roadmaps.

---

## 3. Comprehensive SDLC & Governance Suite Authored:

* **AS-IS & TO-BE Process Maps:** Detailed operational workflows created after hands-on process validation alongside frontline Ops teams.
* **Business Requirement Documents (BRD) & FSDs:** Translating operational logic and exception matrices into structured technical specifications for engineering builds.
* **UAT & Production Sign-Off Records:** Formal execution logs, milestone sign-offs, and sample test case validation docs across Ops and Tech teams.
* **Bot Repository & Absorption Documentation:** Centralised bot registration metadata, estimated decommissioning schedules, and core technology product absorption plans.

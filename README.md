# Portfolio Module 02: Parachute Project Management, End-to-End SDLC Enablement & Ops-Tech Bridge Leadership (JPMorgan Chase)

## Executive Summary:
* **Domain:** Parachute Technical Project Management, SDLC Documentation Architecture, Operations Control Management (OCM) Alignment & RPA Technical Enablement
* **Company Context:** JPMorgan Chase & Co. (Wholesale Payments Operations)
* **Role:** Vice President – Intelligent Automation Lead & Ops-Tech Bridge Liaison
* **Core Value Delivered:** Rescued stalled and high-risk automation initiatives by stepping in as a "Parachute PM." Compressed delivery timelines by 30%–40%, authored complete SDLC documentation suites (BRD, FSD, SDD, End User Guides, Version Control), provided step-by-step guidance to technical teams across AA, UiPath, Alteryx, and Xceptor, and managed full bot lifecycle governance from UAT enablement to production roadmap absorption.

---

## 1. Operational Challenge & Project Bottlenecks:
* **Red-Status & Stalled Initiatives:** Critical automation projects frequently hit roadblocks due to vendor delays, complex system access hurdles, or technical skill gaps.
* **Documentation Gaps & Misalignment:** Mismatches between frontline operational logic and engineering specs caused scope creep, UAT failures, and extended delivery cycles.
* **Environment & Data Deficits:** Lack of realistic test data and restricted UAT access for bot IDs frequently halted technical development.
* **Governance & Handover Gaps:** Inadequate post-production monitoring, missing bot repository registrations, and unclear decommissioning/absorption roadmaps created long-term technical debt.

---

## 2. End-to-End SDLC Enablement & "Parachute PM" Rescue Framework:

### Execution Steps:
* **Step 1: Process Discovery & Requirement Architecture** – Sitting directly with Operations, mapping end-to-end AS-IS/TO-BE workflows, executing process steps in UAT, and authoring precise Business Requirement Documents (BRD).
* **Step 2: Bot Access Sourcing & Infrastructure Enablement** – Sourcing bot IDs and collaborating with competent authorities to secure bot access to specific operational tools within UAT environments.
* **Step 3: Hands-On Technical Support & RPA Guidance** – Guiding engineering teams step-by-step through coding challenges; stepping in directly when teams struggled with complex logic across **Automation Anywhere, UiPath, Alteryx, and Xceptor**[cite: 1].
* **Step 4: UAT Data Provisioning & Execution** – Partnering with Operations to upload real-world test data into UAT, co-conducting step-by-step UAT validation, and securing formal sign-offs at each testing phase.
* **Step 5: Production Testing & Hypercare Monitoring** – Testing bots in Production on sample live cases, securing operational sign-offs, working with the Bot CoE to schedule production deployment, and executing 1–2 weeks of active hypercare monitoring for stability and accuracy.
* **Step 6: Governance, Bot Registration & Roadmap Absorption** – Authoring complete End User Guides, registering bots into the firmwide Bot Repository with estimated decommissioning dates, and aligning solutions with technology roadmaps for long-term product absorption.

---

## 3. Comprehensive SDLC & Governance Suite Authored:

* **AS-IS & TO-BE Process Maps:** Detailed operational workflows created after hands-on process validation alongside frontline Ops teams.
* **Business Requirement Documents (BRD) & FSDs:** Translating operational logic and exception matrices into structured technical specifications for engineering builds.
* **UAT & Production Sign-Off Records:** Formal execution logs, milestone sign-offs, and sample test case validation docs across Ops and Tech teams.
* **End User Guides & Operating Manuals:** Step-by-step user guides, operating flowcharts, and CoE scheduling protocols for seamless operational handoffs.
* **Bot Repository & Absorption Documentation:** Centralized bot registration metadata, estimated decommissioning schedules, and core technology product absorption plans.
